## Design Context

### Users
Cotabby is for everyday macOS users who want fast, local autocomplete across the apps they already use. In the settings window, they are usually making lightweight adjustments rather than learning a complex system, so navigation should be easy to scan and low-effort to understand.

### Brand Personality
Clean, clear, glanceable. The interface should feel calm and direct, with just enough product personality to avoid feeling sterile or heavy.

### Aesthetic Direction
Favor native macOS settings conventions over novelty. Keep the sidebar visually quiet, easy to skim, and structurally obvious at a glance. Avoid decorative chrome, dense grouping, or attention-grabbing accents unless they communicate a real state that needs action.

### Design Principles
1. Make navigation understandable in one quick scan.
2. Preserve a native macOS feel before adding custom flourish.
3. Use emphasis sparingly so important states stand out.
4. Keep spacing and alignment disciplined enough to feel calm.
5. Prefer clarity and speed of recognition over cleverness.
